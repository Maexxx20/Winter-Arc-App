Nordwand

Your Winter Arc. 92 days, your rules, your contract with yourself – from 1 October to New Year’s Eve.

  • A few clear rules – daily or x times a week, check off or track amounts, with an optional reminder per rule.
  • Never miss twice – one shield per week saves a single missed day.
  • Sign it – you give yourself your word. After that, only three changes.
  • History and weekly review – a heatmap of the whole arc, reflection every Sunday.
  • Arc recap – your arc in numbers at the end: days held, longest streak, strongest rule.
  • Before / after – two photos from your journal side by side, kept on your device.
  • Crews – together with friends or your team, with leaderboard, reactions, nudges and weekly challenges.
  • Crew arcs – one person sets the period and rules as a template, everyone signs their own contract, and you see rule by rule who is keeping it up.
  • Automatic check-off – with Apple Health, Health Connect and Strava: steps, workouts, sleep, water, mindfulness.
  • Widgets – day and streak on your iPhone or Android home screen, check off rules right in the widget.
  • An arc for every season – New Year, Spring, Summer and Winter Arc, or your own period.
  • Your data – works without an account, export as CSV or JSON, in English, German, French and Italian.
